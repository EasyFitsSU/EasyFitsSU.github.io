Project description

EasyFits is a 3D digital wardrobe application where users scan their clothes and their body, then create, combine and virtually try on outfits on their silhouette that can be viewed from any angle (360°), using both items they own and items they could buy online.

The problem

Forgotten clothes

Most people own clothes they are not even aware of, so a lot of items stay unused at the back of the wardrobe.

Outfits take effort to test

Seeing what an outfit looks like means changing again and again, which takes time and patience.

Styling is hard

Many people find it difficult to match their clothes together from a styling point of view.

Shops only show models

Online stores show clothes on models. A normal user cannot see how an item looks on them without trying it on physically.

Our solution

EasyFits turns the user's wardrobe and their silhouette into a 3D digital space where they can build outfits, try them on virtually and look at themselves from every angle, without having to physically get dressed or buying the item.

No Generative AI: we use machine learning, computer vision and other mechanisms.

Scan

The users scan their clothes and their bodies to build their personal 3D wardrobes.

Combine

The users can create and mix outfits from their digital wardrobe and view the result inside the platform.

Try on

Trying on items works both with owned products and ones that the user could eventually buy online.

Customer segments

B2CEnd users

Primary

The everyday outfit planner

Who
Gen Z and Millennials, 18 - 35
Problem
Lots of clothes but "nothing to wear"; too much time spent trying outfits on; duplicate or mismatched purchases.
With EasyFits
A digital closet, faster decisions, and 360° try-ons without the mess.
Secondary

The mindful shopper with a small wardrobe

Who
Shoppers aged 22 - 40 who care about sustainability, including minimalists and second-hand buyers
Problem
They want to wear what they already own more often, and to know a new piece will go with it before they buy.
With EasyFits
They can see how a new item looks with their clothes, so they make fewer impulse buys and send back fewer items.

B2BBusiness partners

Fashion e-commerce retailers

Who
Mid-to-large online brands and marketplaces
Problem
High return rates (mostly fit-related) and cart abandonment when shoppers can't picture the item on themselves.
With EasyFits
An integration offering "Try it on your 3D profile" and "See how it matches your wardrobe."

Boutiques and D2C brands

Who
Emerging brands without the budget to build 3D try-on themselves
Problem
Hard to compete with big retailers' interactive experiences; high acquisition and retention costs.
With EasyFits
A ready-to-use 3D try-on for their online shop that helps shoppers feel confident about what they buy, so the brand sells more.

Competition

Digital closet apps

Whering, Acloset, Indyx

What they offer
2D cataloging of clothes, outfit calendars, outfit suggestions and basic 2D avatar try-ons.
Where they fall short
  • Flat images layered on top of each other, with no side or back view.
  • They don't show how a garment sits on a specific body.

Shopping-first try-on tools

Google Virtual Try-On, StyTrix, TryDrobe

What they offer
AI-generated images that place a single garment on a photo of a model or the user.
Where they fall short
  • Disconnected from the clothes the user already owns.
  • One static front image per attempt, which can distort details, with no 360° view.

Enterprise try-on APIs

Revery.ai, Vue.ai, Veesual

What they offer
Try-on widgets that retailers add to their own websites.
Where they fall short
  • B2B only, with no personal wardrobe app for consumers.
  • Costly to implement for small and mid-size brands.

Our advantage

Computer vision

Real scans, not generated images

EasyFits uses computer vision and machine learning to build a 3D model of the user's body and clothes from real scans. We don’t use generative AI, so what the user sees stays consistent and true to the item instead of an invented picture. Every outfit can be viewed in 360° and the user's whole wardrobe stays sorted in one place.

B2B & B2C

One platform for shoppers and stores

Shoppers keep their wardrobe and 3D profile in the EasyFits app, and retailers add the same try-on to their own websites. People can see how a new item looks with the clothes they already own, and partner stores get customers who feel more sure about what they buy.

Cost structure

Technology and cloud

The biggest cost, and it grows with the number of users

  • 3D and computer vision compute: GPU cloud servers that process body and garment scans. This is our largest expense.
  • Storage and CDN: hosting 3D silhouettes and wardrobe items so 360° views load fast.
  • API infrastructure: servers that handle try-on requests from partner websites.

Product development

The work needed to build the product and keep improving it

  • Model training: making the scans more accurate for different body types.
  • Privacy and security: complying with GDPR and storing sensitive body scan data safely.
  • Development tools: software licenses and devices for testing.

Growth and operations

Increases once the product is live

  • User acquisition: social media, influencers and referral programs.
  • Partner onboarding: integration support for retailers.
  • Support: help for app users and partner API maintenance.

Revenue streams

B2BBusiness partners, our main income

Pay per try-on

Online stores pay for each virtual try-on made through the EasyFits plugin, with lower prices at higher volumes.

Monthly plans

Flat plans for store owners (for example Starter, Growth, Enterprise) based on a monthly try-on quota, analytics and support.

3D catalog setup

A fee for turning a brand's 2D product photos into 3D garments that can be tried on.

B2CUsers

Free with ads

A free 3D body scan, a limited wardrobe and basic 360° outfit creation, supported by ads.

One-time upgrades

Paid tiers, bought once, that raise the wardrobe limit and remove ads.
