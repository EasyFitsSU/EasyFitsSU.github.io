What we changed

Based on the interviews, we made six changes to the wireframe:

  1. The wardrobe is now the home screen. Opening the app takes you straight to your clothes.
  2. Outfit suggestions got their own tab. The old home screen (today’s suggestion, more ideas and the style guide) moved to the second tab, where the wardrobe used to be, and is now called Suggestions.
  3. “My outfits” is now “Outfits you created”, so it is clear these are your own outfits and not the suggested ones.
  4. Scanning uses photos instead of video. Clothes and the user's body are scanned from a few guided photos (front, back, side) instead of recording a 360° video. When scanning a piece, a tip reminds you that you don’t need to wear it: just lay it flat or hang it up and fit it in the frame.
  5. Outfits can be filtered by category. “Outfits you created” now has filter chips (Casual, Work, Party, Sport, Summer, Winter) that use the same tags as the wardrobe.
  6. “Scan my body” is easier to find. It used to be a small toggle at the top of the body model screen. It is now the main button, centred at the bottom of the screen, where it is easier to see and reach.

Revised wireframe

The revised wireframe keeps the same two flows as in Milestone 3: Everyday use (19 screens) and First launch (4 screens), with the changes above applied.
